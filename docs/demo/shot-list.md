# The shot list

Every shot in the video, in the order the video shows them. Each row names its beat in [`script.md`](script.md), its screen (`terminal`, `web` or `split`: a terminal beside the browser), what to run or open on the `demo` stage, what must already be true, and what to use if it fails live. [`rehearsal.md`](rehearsal.md) has one checkbox per shot; record in the order under "Recording order", not the table's.

The stage's two origins:

- the platform, for git and the CLI: `https://platform-demo.oscargabriel.workers.dev`;
- the web: `https://web-demo.oscargabriel.workers.dev`. Its `/` is the landing (what the platform is, a link to the open asks, the gate's refusal as git prints it, the projects to read), and a signed-in visitor at `/` goes on to `/home`; the front page, the open asks across projects, is at `/asks`. No shot opens on `/`.

People on screen are the fictional examples' people. Each terminal acts as one of them through a CLI profile, chosen with `export MYR_PROFILE=<handle>`; git's credential helper reads the same profile, so no token is ever typed, shown or put in a URL. The profiles are logged in before the take (rehearsal pre-flight). Clones live under `~/demo/`.

## Recording order

The video's order is not the order to record in. Two things set it: format-ordinal is held for newcomers, and a person with a landing in datekit is no newcomer, so bram's sealed-check answer (S17, S18) must land before his zero-install answer (S03); and the swarm (S12) changes what the front page and Today show.

1. On the fresh seed, read-only: S01, S02, S07, S08, S09, S16, S19.
2. The layer: S15, S17, S18.
3. Zero install: S05, then S03, S04, S06.
4. The swarm: S12, with S13 and S14 open beside it; S10 and S11 come from this take.
5. After the swarm: S20, S21, S22, S23, S24, S25, S26.

No deploy between the first take and the last.
